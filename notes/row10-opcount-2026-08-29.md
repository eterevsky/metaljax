## The map, validated against the phase-2 module dump

The captured `jit__generate_jit` (26-layer MoE scan body, ~700 tape
entries/layer) decomposed not as the plan expected.  The single biggest
line was invisible from op counts alone: **maxtext rebuilds the entire
[163840, 32] complex rope frequency table from iota, twice per layer**
(~104 module ops), then gathers ONE row of it — 52 rebuilds of a 40 MB
table per decoded token, worth ~76 ms of the 108.  The MLA attention
machinery (~80 ops/layer) and the router bookkeeping (~55) were real but
second-order; the expert `gather_mm`s and the ~5 GB/token of genuine
weight streaming (~10 ms) were already at their floor.

## Engine fixes landed (all engine-side, each gated off-able)

1. **Tape post-passes** (`metal_lowering.cc`, run in `Finish` ahead of
   liveness, per frame — while bodies included):
   * `CsePass` (`METALJAX_CSE=0` disables): structural CSE over pure
     region-free entries — one forward pass with a slot remap, so chains
     collapse transitively; constants merge earlier, in `LowerConstant`,
     by their MLIR-uniqued attribute (pointer equality is value equality,
     so an inlined callee's constants merge across call sites without
     reading data).  The SECOND rope table is an exact structural copy of
     the first and follows it out entry by entry, gather included; the
     router's three identical `cumsum` splices merge the same way.
   * `FoldConstants` (`METALJAX_FOLD=0`): argument-independent subgraphs
     of a REGION frame evaluate once at lower time (through a throwaway
     `Program` with drop lists) and become payload constants.  Region
     frames only: a while body REPLAYS its tape, while a 0-arg @main (a
     checkpoint restore) is nothing but argument-independent ops that
     must not be materialized.  Boundary classification with a broadcast
     barrier (materializing a broadcast would trade a zero-stride view
     for real bytes; its input folds instead) and byte caps
     (`METALJAX_FOLD_MAX_MB` 96 per payload, `METALJAX_FOLD_TOTAL_MB` 256
     per frame — an oversized boundary keeps the entry and folds ITS
     inputs).  Folded slots are marked constant-views and the cv taint is
     re-propagated, so the no-alias output-copy rule still fires behind a
     reshape.  Any evaluation failure keeps the literal tape.
   * Splat-broadcast fold: `broadcast_in_dim(splat constant)` lowers as
     ONE payload entry (a splat's device form is a zero-stride view
     whatever its shape), deduped per (attr, shape); rank-0 results stay
     with the %.7g literal rule.
   * `DcePass`: pure entries nothing reads are dropped (what CSE/fold
     leave behind).
2. **MLA multi-span decode attention recognizer** (`metal_mla.cc`, opcode
   `metaljax.mla_sdpa`, handler in `runtime/emits.cc`;
   `METALJAX_MLA=0` disables).  maxtext's MLA decode computes each cache
   span (prefill, ar) as a masked softmax with running max/sum and joins
   them with the flash renormalization — which IS the softmax over the
   concatenated scores.  The fingerprint (both scores dots, the
   segment-id mask trees through the outlined `_where` callees, the
   per-span max/sum chains, the combine algebra) is matched exactly, ~74
   ops absorbed per layer; the emit concats the spans, builds the
   additive mask from the segment ids (the same 0 / -2.38e38 the matched
   chain selects) and calls `fast::scaled_dot_product_attention` — whose
   vector kernel supports the MLA 192/128 head geometry (q_len 1, gqa
   1x1 <= 32).  Declines whole unless the use-count fixpoint absorbs
   every matched op.
3. **RMS-norm recognizer** (`metal_norm.cc`, opcode `metaljax.rms_norm`;
   `METALJAX_NORM=0`).  The ~13-op spelled-out norm (upcast, square,
   sum/N, +eps, rsqrt, scale, downcast, batching-dot weight apply,
   transpose) becomes `fast::rms_norm(x, w, eps)`.  Matched 7x on row 10
   (3 per MoE layer incl. the 512-wide KV norm, 3 dense-body, 1 final).

## Two latent bugs the suite caught (both fixed, both pre-existing in
## narrower forms)

The first full execute_test run failed 4 ways, all one root cause: a
payload stored as a LAZY mx graph.  (a) mx::compile's fusion walks an
unevaluated movement chain down to its one-element buffer, and
`size() == 1 && !has_primitive()` (mlx compile.cpp:972) then inlines it
into the fused kernel source as a %.7g literal — the historic 1-ULP
constant-baking bug, amplified ~400x by tan's pole in the
ill-conditioned suite case (measured 2.4147e-3 = exactly the
both-constants-baked prediction; reproducible whenever ANY prior metal
executable had run).  (b) a lazy node records the CREATING thread's
thread-unsafe stream (metal_stream.cc binds one per entering thread),
which `get_command_encoder` cannot resolve from another thread — the
threaded-execute failure.  LowerConstant's numel>1 splat views carried
the same two hazards since the splat path shipped; the splat-broadcast
fold made them ubiquitous.  Fix: every stored payload is settled to a
LEAF at lowering (one batched eval at the top of Finish; no primitive,
no stream, and a size>1 view can never be scalar-inlined), size-1
splat-broadcast folds are skipped, and a fold whose payload is a size-1
rank>=1 f32 that does not round-trip (or size-1 complex) keeps the
literal tape.  AbsorbedSlot's lazy zero got the same settling.

## Token-stream / numerics disclosure

* NO token stream changed anywhere in this campaign: row 10 matches its
  gate record, rows 11/14 match their phase-2 records, on both the
  pre-fix and the frozen final binary.
* CSE + constant folding are bit-exact on the eager decode path (the
  fold evaluates with the same unfused kernels the per-iteration body
  ran); the standalone check digits are identical to the HEAD binary's.
  On COMPILED paths a folded value replaces one computed inside a fused
  kernel, where Metal's compiler may contract differently — 1-ULP class.
* The MLA rewrite moves the probabilities from the literal bf16
  max-subtract/exp chain into the sdpa kernel's f32 arithmetic, and the
  rms_norm rewrite rounds `x * rsqrt` once instead of twice — both the
  documented fused-kernel class: tolerance-level vs CPU (standalone
  whole-module check max_norm 9.3e-03 at bf16), greedy near-ties MAY
  flip on other prompts even though none did here.

## Remaining gap attribution (vs mlx-lm 10.5)

At ~29 ms/exec standalone: ~10 ms device work at the genuine streaming
floor (~4.8 GB/token: 6 live experts x 3 x 26 ~ 2.7 GB, shared/attn/
logits the rest), ~15 ms host-side mx graph construction of the ~10k
remaining nodes (or, compiled, the same cost as MLX replay/encode), ~4
ms loop sync/misc.  The floor is now launch-and-build-bound, not
wrong-path-bound.  Next campaign, priced: a prepared-replay executor
(build the mx graph once, rebind buffers per token) or a persistent
decode kernel would attack the ~15 ms; the streaming floor bounds
everything above ~10-12 ms/tok on this machine.
